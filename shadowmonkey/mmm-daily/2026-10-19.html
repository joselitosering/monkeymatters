Graham Sanctions Against Russia and Iran Act” targeting Russia’s energy trade — a pause, not a repeal — which eased oil (WTI and Brent both down roughly 0.7–1.6% across this morning’s premarket snapshots) and came alongside Treasury yields that have already eased to 5.09% on the 10-year (Oct 16 close, per FMP) from the Oct 14 CPI-shock peak above 5.32%. Related AI-infrastructure names followed: Super Micro +3.5%, CoreWeave +2.9%, Nvidia +1.5%.
