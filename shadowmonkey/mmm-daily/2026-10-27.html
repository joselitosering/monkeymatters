No independent source in this pull could confirm the reported 10% Brazilian real decline some outlets flagged as “possible” rather than observed. 🔍 Spotlight
Brazil's Surprise Lula Win Keeps Crushing ADRs
Lula won Sunday's runoff with 51.8% of the vote against Flávio Bolsonaro's 48.2% — the first time a candidate has ever lost the first round and gone on to win a Brazilian presidential runoff, and a result prediction markets had bet against (CNBC, Yahoo/TIKR). Petrobras fell roughly 15% and MercadoLibre 12% on Monday as the market priced in another term of the current capital-allocation approach at Petrobras, where capex has more than doubled since 2022 while the dividend payout ratio fell from 94% to under 40% of free cash flow. The rout hasn't stopped: today's FMP data shows XP Inc. down 48.36%, Banco Bradesco down 28.01%, StoneCo down 27.44%, and Azul down 32.61%, with leveraged Brazil ETFs (BRZU, UBR) down roughly 39% each on top of that. No source in this pull could confirm a reported 10% real decline some outlets flagged as merely “possible.”
Celestica's Blowout Beat and Meta's $97.5B AI Bet
Celestica (CLS) reported Q3 revenue of $5.83B, up 83% year over year, with non-GAAP EPS of $3.30 versus $1.58 a year ago — both beating the top end of prior guidance — and raised FY2026 revenue guidance to $21B while issuing a first-ever 2027 target of $40B; shares were indicated up roughly 15.8% pre-market (TradingKey). Separately, Meta raised its planned equity financing to $97.5B (a $25B common stock offering at $625/share plus $12.5B of mandatory convertible preferred and an ATM program up to $60B) to fund AI-infrastructure buildout, with Nvidia committing to subscribe $3B of the raise and AMD and Broadcom expressing interest in up to $5B combined. That's real capital, not just a press release, backing the AI-infrastructure buildout — a direct counter to the “AI-capex-doubt” narrative that drove the Oct. 22 Nasdaq rout. Meta shares themselves were “largely flat” pre-market on the news.
A Hawkish Bond Market Ahead of a Loaded Wednesday
The 10-year yield has pushed back up to essentially 5.00% (FMP treasury-rates, Oct. 26), up from 4.96% just four sessions earlier (Oct. 22) — a real repricing even as equities stayed calm Monday. An unverified FXStreet sidebar headline (“Federal Reserve: December hike expectations — BNY”) raises the possibility markets are now entertaining a December hike rather than a cut; this report could not independently confirm that claim beyond the single teaser, but it's directionally consistent with the yield move. Wednesday stacks the FOMC decision (2pm ET, consensus expects a hold per TradingKey) directly against Microsoft and Alphabet earnings the same afternoon, with Amazon following Thursday — a genuine binary-risk pileup that a calm VIX (17.9) may be underpricing.
