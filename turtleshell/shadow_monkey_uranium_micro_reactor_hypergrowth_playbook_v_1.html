Prospectus: Nuclear Fuel Cycle + Modular Reactors

We target a structural supply‑demand imbalance in uranium alongside commercialization of small/modular and micro‑reactors (SMR/MMR). Electrification, AI datacenters, and reshoring drive firm baseload demand; mine restarts and secondary supply alone are insufficient in tight cycles. Barbell exposure: fuel‑cycle torque (miners, conversion, enrichment/HALEU) + SMR enablement (OEMs, component manufacturers, EPCs, operators).

  • Economics: >90% capacity factors for baseload + premium PPAs for islanded loads (datacenters, industrial heat).
  • Catalysts: Utility term‑contracting waves, mine restart FIDs, HALEU awards, OEM design certifications, government stockpile actions.
  • Bottlenecks: Enrichment capacity (esp. HALEU), conversion limits, licensing lead‑times, factory fabrication ramp.

How We Trade It — Strategy Guide

  • Event‑first: Enter on funded contracts, design/site approvals, or restart FIDs; avoid headline‑only MoUs.
  • Defined risk: Use verticals/diagonals on liquid names; cash‑secured puts at preset buy levels; avoid naked short gamma in microcaps.
  • Liquidity rule: Minimum ADV and spreads required; stagger fills (L1/L2/Breakout) via tickets below.
  • Time stops: If catalyst window slips by T+2 weeks with no confirmation, trim or exit; recycle capital.
