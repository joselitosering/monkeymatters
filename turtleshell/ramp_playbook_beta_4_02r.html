Build a diversified, high‑convexity portfolio across six secular curves (AI/HPC, Quantum, Blockchain rails, Space exploration, Robotics/Bionics, and Advanced Manufacturing) with disciplined risk governance. We maximize upside through leaders and mid‑caps while containing left‑tail risk via defined‑risk options, staged entries, and mechanical rebalancing.

Scenario A — Full Conversion Now

  • Action: Sell 100% HIVE via limit/TWAP and deploy to target sleeves immediately (40/30/30 staging).
  • Pros: Instant diversification; removes single‑name risk; clean alignment with playbook.
  • Torque add (optional): Long MBT micro‑BTC futures (1–2 contracts) to restore crypto beta.
  • Risk circuit: MBT per‑contract stop = max(1.5× monthly option income, BTC −5%); halve size if VIX > 28.

Scenario B — Staged Exit (60/40 Stub)

  • Action: Sell 60% now; keep 40% HIVE for 60–90 days.
  • Income overlay (L1): Monthly covered calls on ~20–40% of total shares (Δ≈0.20; 30–45 DTE). Roll at 50–70% profit or T‑21.
  • On L2 approval: Convert part of the stub to LEAPS Δ0.70–0.80 (18–24 mo) + short monthly calls Δ0.20–0.30 (diagonal; sell on ~50% of LEAPS).
  • Directional ladders: 3/6/9‑mo bull call verticals (buy Δ0.55–0.65 / sell Δ0.20–0.30), budget ≤0.8% each; TP 50–75%; hard stop −60%.
  • Wheel later (L2): Sell cash‑secured puts Δ~0.20 at buy‑levels; on assignment, sell covered calls.

Scenario C — Keep HIVE Heavier with Hedges

  • Action: Retain 70–100% HIVE; monetize with covered calls and defined‑risk spreads; keep diversified sleeves smaller.
  • Protection: Optional collars into earnings or halving events (sell OTM call Δ~0.15 / buy OTM put Δ~0.15 for small net cost).
  • Torque: Add 1–2 MBT when BTC > 20‑DMA and fees rising; cut on 20‑DMA break.
  • When to choose: Only if you explicitly want lottery‑ticket upside and tolerate deep drawdowns.

Scenario guide — Choose A for clean transition, B for convexity + income, C for max torque with guardrails. All options subject to IRA permissions (L1: covered calls; L2: spreads/diagonals/CSPs).

Risk, Protection & Repair

Portfolio‑Level

  • Kill‑switch: If portfolio DD ≥ −20% and VIX > 28 → close small/spec options, halve MBT futures.
  • Rebalance: Quarterly, or sleeve drift ±35%.
  • Cash buffer: Keep 3–5% to fund rolls and pullbacks.

Protection

  • Leaders: far‑OTM covered calls (Δ 0.10–0.20) for income; protective collars into earnings for COIN.
  • Specs: debit call verticals only; hard stops −30% (−35% for microcaps).

Repair

  • Roll short calls at 50–70% max profit; roll up/out if Δ > 0.35.
  • For losers with intact thesis: add on −5 to −8% pullbacks up to cap; otherwise rotate to sleeve leader.

Trade Tickets & Buy Conditions

Staged entries per ticker. Use GTC limits. For OTC ADRs, limit‑only. Values are rule‑based to remain durable.

Ticket Rules

  • L1: Buy 40% allocation at limit = 0.995 × last (or bid mid for OTC).
  • L2: Buy 30% at limit = 0.95 × last (pullback add) if not filled.
  • Brk: Buy 30% on breakout with stop = 1.03 × last and limit = 1.035 × last.
  • Stops/Trails: per‑ticker rules above; hard stops for specs; leader trails 12–18%.
