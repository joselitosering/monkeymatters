Appendix A — Formulas & Sizing

Donchian Channels (offset=1): H_n = max(High[-n..-1]), L_n = min(Low[-n..-1])
ATR (Wilder, n=20): N_t = N_{t-1} + (TR_t − N_{t-1})/n; TR_t = max(High−Low, |High−PrevClose|, |Low−PrevClose|)
$ per 1N/contract: N × PV × Mult (ES: $50/pt)
Contracts per unit: floor( Equity × risk% / $1N )
Initial stop: Entry ± 2×Nⁱ (execution timeframe)
Add levels: Entry ± {0.5, 1.0, 1.5}×Nⁱ
Heat (portfolio): Σ UnitRisk% across open units and sleeves ≤ 4–6%

Appendix B — Pseudocode (Signals & Orders)

# Daily pass (EOD)
for market in universe:
    H20, L20 = donchian(high, low, n=20, offset=1)
    H55, L55 = donchian(high, low, n=55, offset=1)
    Nd = atr_wilder(close, high, low, n=20)
    store(levels={H20,L20,H55,L55,Nd})

# Session runner (5m)
Ni = atr_wilder_5m(close5, high5, low5, n=20)
if close5 > H20_prev and confirm_close():
    place_stop_buy(price=H20_prev + tick)
if entry_filled:
    stop = entry - 2*Ni
    adds = [entry + k*0.5*Ni for k in (1,2,3)]
    oco_stop(stop)
    stage_add_stops(adds)
exit_if = (low5 < L10i if long else high5 > H10i) or eod_time()

# confirm_close(): require 5m body ≥ 0.2×Ni beyond the rail

Appendix D — Daily/Weekly Checklists

Purpose. Checklists compress the entire program into repeatable behaviors that reduce variance and decision fatigue. By standardizing what is checked and when, we preserve rule‑quality execution even when emotions or market noise spike. Items are ordered to establish context first (rails, Nᵈ/Nⁱ), then risk (UnitRisk, heat), then orders (entries, stops, adds), then review and logging.

Daily — Pre‑Open (T‑30 to T‑5). Compute H20/L20 and H55/L55 with offset=1 on the daily; record Nᵈ. Initialize a 5‑minute session feed and compute Nⁱ. Translate $1N by product (e.g., ES=$50/pt). Set UnitRisk (0.25–0.50% eval; 0.5–1.0% live). Check heat cap (≤4–6%). Draft entry triggers (STOPs at rails with a 1‑tick buffer) and pre‑stage OCO catastrophic stops at 2×Nⁱ. If Balanced, budget Shell credit; if Trend, budget Shell debit. Publish the morning plan.

Daily — During Session. Trade S1 only when a 5‑minute bar closes beyond the rail (or body ≥0.2×Nⁱ beyond it). Place adds at ±0.5×Nⁱ increments up to cap. Respect two‑strike rule (stop after two −1R attempts). In stress (Nⁱ ≥ 90th pct), halve UnitRisk and prefer micros; in balanced tapes, avoid over‑trading and let Shell carry the day.

Daily — Close. Flatten futures and cancel staged orders unless you deliberately hand risk to an options structure (collar, put spread, debit vertical). Export fills; log Nᵈ/Nⁱ, rail touches, entries, exits, and R‑multiples. Write a one‑line post‑mortem: "Did I obey the plan?"

Weekly — Review. Reconcile slippage vs %N, distribution of R by regime, Shell contribution, and adherence violations. Refresh parameters only if they drifted (no weekly curve‑fitting). Update runbooks and incident notes.

Appendix E — Glossary (Program Lexicon)

TermDefinition
S1 / S2Turtle breakouts: S1 uses H20/L20 with exits on L10/H10; S2 uses H55/L55 with exits on L20/H20. S2 trades fewer, larger swings.
Donchian Channels (H20/L20/H55/L55)Highest high / lowest low over n prior bars with offset=1 (exclude current bar); defines breakout rails.
N (ATR(20))Volatility unit (Wilder). Nᵈ=daily ATR; Nⁱ=intraday ATR (e.g., 5‑minute). All stops, adds, and sizing are expressed in N.
$1NDollars per 1N for one contract: Nⁱ × point value × multiplier (ES=$50/pt; MES=$5/pt).
UnitRiskDollar risk per unit: Equity × risk%. Contracts per unit = floor(UnitRisk ÷ $1N).
HeatPortfolio risk budget: Σ(UnitRisk) ÷ Equity. Target ≤ 4–6% across sleeves.
R‑multipleProfit or loss in units of initial risk (R). Standardizes outcome evaluation.
Expectancyh×W − (1−h)×L in R (hit rate, average win, average loss). Program‑level edge metric.
Kelly / Fractional KellyOptimal growth fraction given edge; applied fractionally (0.25–0.50×) to bound drawdowns under uncertainty.
MAR / SharpeMAR=CAGR/Max DD; Sharpe=excess return ÷ volatility. Program KPIs.
VWAPVolume‑Weighted Average Price; used as a magnet in ranges.
ADXAverage Directional Index; structural trend strength. Flat ADX implies balance.
Market InternalsTICK (upticks−downticks), TRIN (Arms Index), Breadth (adv/dec) — context filters, not triggers.
IV / IV Rank (IVR) / HVImplied volatility, its rank vs 1‑year range, and historical volatility; drive option structure choice.
GreeksΔ (delta, directional), Γ (gamma, convexity), Θ (theta, decay), ν (vega, vol sensitivity).
Skew / Term StructurePattern of IV across strikes / expiries; informs spreads, BWBs, calendars.
Structures — CreditIron Condor, Iron Fly, Short Vertical, Broken‑Wing Butterfly (reduced debit on one wing).
Structures — DebitLong OTM options, Debit Vertical, Calendar/Diagonal (time/vol edge), Backspread, LEAPS.
CollarLong underlying (or delta) + protective put financed with short call; defines downside.
Confirmation FilterRequire 5‑minute close with bar body ≥0.2×Nⁱ beyond the rail to reduce whipsaw.
L10ⁱ / H10ⁱ / L20ⁱ / H20ⁱIntraday Donchian exits on 10/20 bars for S1/S2 management.
Order TypesSTOP‑MKT (stressed), STOP‑LMT with protection (balanced); OCO for stop pairing; DAY vs GTC.
Slippage / Impact / LatencyExecution frictions; budgeted as a fraction of Nⁱ; monitored in logs.
Kill‑SwitchOne command that cancels adds, tightens stops to market, and flattens on breach of daily stop.
IdempotencyAction semantics that avoid duplicate orders on retries (same request yields same state).
Daemon / Cron / WebhookSession process; scheduled daily jobs; event triggers from charts to order layer.
Append‑Only LogImmutable audit trail (RFC3339 timestamps) for fills, orders, and state transitions.
Continuous Futures (Back‑Adjusted)Splice contracts to a synthetic series; back‑adjust for roll gaps (Panama/ratio methods).
Point Value / MultiplierContract economics (e.g., ES $50/pt) required for $1N computation.
Drawdown ThrottlesAutomated risk cuts at −3R, −5R, −7R; micros‑only or flat policies.
Two‑Strike RuleStop trading after two −1R attempts; prevents revenge trades.
Margin — SPAN / Reg‑T / Portfolio MarginRisk‑based margining frameworks affecting position sizing and Shell selection.
Tax — §1256 / §475(f) / TTS60/40 blended capital gains (1256), mark‑to‑market election (475(f)), Trader Tax Status; seek professional guidance.
Backtest / Walk‑ForwardIn‑sample fit vs out‑of‑sample validation; guard against overfitting.
Risk‑of‑RuinProbability of capital impairment under given edge and risk; reduced by heat caps and strike rules.
z‑ScoreStandardized deviation (e.g., VIX z); used to gate stressed regimes.
